Lumi privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Lumi is a photo and video editor that runs entirely on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no ads and no servers.

What Lumi does with your data

  • Photos, videos and camera: Lumi reads the photos and videos you choose, uses the camera when you open the Camera tab, and saves results to your photo library when you tap Save. All editing and AI processing happens on your device.
  • No collection: Lumi does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data, and it does not track you.
  • Drafts and recipes are stored only on your device, inside the app.
  • Network: Lumi does not need the internet to edit. It makes no network requests with your content.

Permissions

  • Camera to take photos.
  • Photo library (add) to save your edited photos and videos.
  • Microphone to record sound with videos.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/hlum/Lumi.
